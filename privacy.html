Privacy Policy隐私政策

Effective October 24, 2026生效日期：2026 年 10 月 24 日

This policy explains what information EORACCO collects through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

本政策说明 EORACCO 通过本网站收集哪些信息、如何使用，以及您享有的选择和权利。

What we collect我们收集哪些信息

When you send an inquiry through our contact form, we receive the details you enter: your name, email, business name, phone number, the services you are interested in, your message, any plan you built on our pricing page, and the language you were viewing the site in.

We do not use analytics, advertising pixels or tracking cookies on this website.

当您通过网站联系表单提交咨询时，我们会收到您填写的信息：姓名、邮箱、公司名称、电话、感兴趣的服务、留言、您在价格页面组合的方案，以及您浏览网站时使用的语言。

本网站不使用任何分析工具、广告像素或追踪类 Cookie。

How we use it我们如何使用这些信息

We use your details only to reply to your inquiry, prepare a proposal or quote, and, if you become a client, to provide and bill for our services. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

我们只会用这些信息来回复您的咨询、准备方案或报价；如果您成为客户，也会用于提供服务和开具账单。我们不会出售您的个人信息，也不会把它分享给任何人用于广告。

Service providers我们使用的服务商

A few trusted providers help us run this website and handle inquiries. They process information only on our behalf:

  • Google (Gmail, Google Sheets and Apps Script) receives and stores your inquiry.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver fonts and code libraries, so your browser connects to them when a page loads.
  • Stripe processes online payments. Card details go straight to Stripe; we never see or store your full card number.

If we work together on a project, we may share the details a contractor needs to complete that work, under confidentiality.

以下服务商协助我们运营网站和处理咨询，它们只会代表我们处理信息：

  • Google（Gmail、Google 表格、Apps Script）：接收并保存您的咨询。
  • GitHub Pages：托管本网站，可能保留常规服务器日志（例如 IP 地址）。
  • Google Fonts、jsDelivr：提供字体和代码库，页面加载时您的浏览器会连接它们。
  • Stripe：处理在线付款。银行卡信息直接提交给 Stripe，我们看不到也不保存您的完整卡号。

如果我们开展项目合作，可能会在保密前提下，把完成工作所需的信息提供给合作方。

Cookies and local storageCookie 与本地存储

We do not set tracking cookies. Your browser's local storage remembers two things on your own device: your language choice (English, bilingual or Chinese) and the plan you are building on the pricing page. You can clear these at any time in your browser settings.

Our website does not respond to Do Not Track signals because we do not track visitors across sites.

我们不设置任何追踪类 Cookie。浏览器的本地存储只会在您自己的设备上记住两件事：您选择的语言（英文、双语或中文），以及您在价格页面组合的方案。您可以随时在浏览器设置中清除。

由于我们不会跨网站追踪访客，因此网站不需要响应“请勿追踪”（Do Not Track）信号。

How long we keep it保存期限

We keep inquiries for up to 24 months so we can follow up, and then delete them. For clients, we keep project and billing records for as long as we work together and for as long as the law requires afterward.

咨询信息最多保存 24 个月，便于后续跟进，之后会删除。客户的项目和账单记录会在合作期间保存，合作结束后按法律要求的期限保存。

Your choices and rights您的权利

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. California residents have these rights under California law, and we extend them to everyone. We will not treat you differently for making a request.

Email eoraccoofficial@gmail.com from the address you used to contact us, and we will respond within 45 days.

您可以要求我们告知持有您的哪些个人信息，也可以要求更正或删除。加州居民依据加州法律享有这些权利，我们对所有访客一视同仁，提出请求不会让您受到任何区别对待。

请用您当初联系我们的邮箱发信至 eoraccoofficial@gmail.com，我们会在 45 天内回复。

Security信息安全

We keep inquiries in a password-protected account that only we can access. No method of sending or storing data online is completely secure, so please do not send passwords, payment card numbers or other sensitive details through the contact form.

咨询信息保存在受密码保护、仅限我们访问的账号中。但任何线上传输或存储方式都无法做到绝对安全，请不要通过联系表单发送密码、银行卡号等敏感信息。

Children儿童隐私

本网站面向企业客户，不针对 13 岁以下儿童，我们不会有意收集儿童的信息。
