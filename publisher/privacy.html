EORACCO Publisher Privacy PolicyEORACCO Publisher 隐私政策

Effective date: November 7, 2026 · 生效日期：2026 年 11 月 7 日

EORACCO Publisher (the "Service") is a social media publishing tool operated by EORACCO ("we", "us"). It lets EORACCO and the clients we work with connect their own social media accounts, prepare posts, and publish or schedule them to Facebook, Instagram, Threads, TikTok and YouTube. This policy explains what information the Service accesses, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies only to the Service; our general website privacy policy is at eoracco.com/privacy.html.

The Service uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube channel you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

1. Information we access and collect我们访问和收集的信息

When you connect a social media account, you sign in directly with that platform and choose which permissions to grant. We never see or store your platform password. Depending on the platform, we receive:

  • Account information: your account or channel ID, display name, username and profile picture, so we can show which account a post will go to.
  • Access tokens: the authorization tokens the platform issues, which let the Service publish on your behalf.
  • Content you provide: the videos, images, captions, hashtags, cover images and scheduling times you add to a post.
  • Publishing results: the post ID, link and status (scheduled, published or failed) the platform returns, and basic post metrics where the platform provides them.

We do not access your private messages, contacts or friend lists, and we do not collect information from people who view or interact with your posts.

2. How we use information我们如何使用这些信息

  • To publish or schedule the posts you create, to the accounts you choose, at the times you choose.
  • To show you your connected accounts, your post calendar and whether each post succeeded.
  • To keep the Service secure and to fix problems.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or use it to build profiles of you. We only send content to a platform after you have reviewed the post and chosen to publish or schedule it.

3. How we share information我们如何共享信息

  • With the platforms you post to: your content and post settings are sent to Meta (Facebook, Instagram, Threads), TikTok or Google (YouTube) only to publish the posts you requested.
  • With our hosting provider: the Service runs on servers we rent; the provider stores data on our behalf and may not use it for its own purposes.
  • When required by law: if we must comply with a valid legal request.

We do not share information with any other third party.

4. Storage and retention存储与保存期限

  • Access tokens and account information are kept while the account is connected and are deleted within 7 days after you disconnect the account or revoke access.
  • Uploaded media and captions are kept until the post is published plus up to 30 days, so failed posts can be retried, and are then deleted.
  • Data received from YouTube API Services is refreshed or deleted at least every 30 days, as YouTube's policies require.

5. Your choices: revoking access and deleting data您的选择：撤销授权与删除数据

  • Disconnect in the Service: remove an account from the Service at any time; this deletes its tokens.
  • Revoke from the platform:
    • Google / YouTube: Google security settings
    • Facebook / Instagram: Settings → Apps and Websites in your Facebook or Instagram account
    • Threads: Settings → Account → Website permissions
    • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services
  • Data deletion request: email eoraccoofficial@gmail.com with the subject "EORACCO Publisher data deletion" and the account you connected. We delete all data the Service holds for that account within 30 days and confirm by email.

6. Security信息安全

Connections to the Service use HTTPS. Access tokens are stored on our server, access is limited to the people who operate the Service, and accounts are protected with two-factor authentication where available. No system is perfectly secure, so please contact us right away if you believe your account has been misused.

7. Children儿童隐私

The Service is for businesses and adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Changes and contact政策更新与联系方式

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, notify connected users. Questions or requests: eoraccoofficial@gmail.com.
